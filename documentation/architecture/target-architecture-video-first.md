# Target Architecture — Video-First, Retention-Driven Production

**Status:** Authoritative Target Architecture  
**Governing Decisions:** ADR-0026, ADR-0027, ADR-0028, ADR-0029, ADR-0030, ADR-0031  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

NarrativeX is transitioning from a legacy image-first production model (`Story → VisualBeat → Image → Ken Burns → Timeline`) to an end-to-end **video-first, retention-driven** architecture:

```text
Source / Chapter
      ↓
Story Director (Vertex Gemini 3.8 Flash)
      ↓
Character & World Bible
      ↓
Retention Engine (HookPlan & RetentionMap)
      ↓
Scene & VisualBeat (Dramatic Intent)
      ↓
Shot Sequence
      ↓
Shot Director
      ↓
Generation Router (T2V, I2V, First/Last Frame, Multi-Keyframe, Extend, Retake)
      ↓
LTX-2.5 (Moving Footage Takes)
      ↓
Video QA (Failure Taxonomy & Reason-Aware Retry)
      ↓
Selected Take (In/Out Trimming)
      ↓
Editing Director (EditDecisionList)
      ↓
VieNeu TTS / Narration / SFX / Subtitle Alignment
      ↓
FFmpeg Local Assembly (Moving Video Cuts, 720p/24 FPS)
      ↓
Final Master & Packaging
      ↓
Retention Feedback (Post-Publish Analytics Loop)
```

---

## 2. Core Pillars

### 2.1 Story Intelligence & Retention Engine
- **Story Director**: Breaks source text into dramatic beats rather than image prompts.
- **HookPlan**: Formulates the initial viewer promise, conflict, curiosity question, visual hook, and withheld information to secure the first 30 seconds of audience attention.
- **RetentionMap**: Tracks narrative tension, curiosity loops, emotional progression, open questions, and resolved payoffs across the timeline.
- **Attention Event Detector**: Monitors the frequency of narrative, visual, and auditory shifts (`NEW_INFORMATION`, `CONFLICT`, `REVEAL`, `LOCATION_CHANGE`, `REACTION`, `PAYOFF`). Gaps exceeding threshold durations trigger `PACING_RISK` warnings before expensive generation.

### 2.2 Shot as the Atomic Production Unit
- `VisualBeat` represents a **dramatic moment**, not a single visual asset.
- A `VisualBeat` maps to a `ShotSequence` containing 1 to N `Shot` instances.
- A `Shot` is the atomic boundary for:
  - Generation intent (Narrative purpose, start state, action, end state, composition, camera, motions).
  - Generation strategy selection.
  - Compute admission, leasing, and retry tracking.
  - Multi-take management.
  - Video QA validation.

### 2.3 Image Generation Reassigned to References and Keyframes
- primary moving video is never generated by applying Ken Burns pan/zoom to still images.
- Image generation services (e.g., ComfyUI RealVisXL) are strictly auxiliary, producing:
  1. `CHARACTER_REFERENCE` (consistent identity, wardrobe, facial expressions).
  2. `LOCATION_REFERENCE` (environment, architecture, lighting).
  3. `START_FRAME` / `END_FRAME` (for FIRST_LAST_FRAME conditioned video generation).
  4. `KEYFRAME` (for multi-keyframe video generation).
  5. `THUMBNAIL` / `POSTER` (for publishing packaging).

### 2.4 Model-Agnostic Generation Routing
The `GenerationRouter` abstracts underlying model providers and selects the optimal video generation strategy:
- `TEXT_TO_VIDEO` (T2V): Wide environments, b-roll, atmospheric establishing shots without recurring character identity constraints.
- `IMAGE_TO_VIDEO` (I2V): Character-centric shots, dialogue, emotional close-ups anchored by approved character reference images.
- `FIRST_LAST_FRAME`: Transitions requiring exact start and end compositions or precise action paths.
- `MULTI_KEYFRAME`: Complex choreographies requiring key progress milestones.
- `VIDEO_EXTEND`: Extending continuous camera moves or sustained physical actions.
- `VIDEO_RETAKE`: Targeted motion or lighting adjustments over an existing take without full regeneration.

### 2.5 Multi-Take Pipeline & Reason-Aware Video QA
- Generation output never directly populates the edit timeline.
- Every generation attempt creates a `Take` tied to the parent `Shot`.
- `VideoQA` evaluates each take against an explicit failure taxonomy:
  `FACE_IDENTITY`, `CHARACTER_CONSISTENCY`, `ANATOMY`, `MOTION`, `TEMPORAL_ARTIFACT`, `CAMERA`, `COMPOSITION`, `PROMPT_ADHERENCE`, `CONTINUITY`, `DURATION`, `TECHNICAL_OUTPUT`.
- If a take fails, retries are reason-aware: prompts, reference weights, or motion settings are adjusted according to the failure mode. Blind resubmissions with identical configurations are forbidden.

### 2.6 Take Selection & Editing Director
- The timeline consumes `SelectedTake` items.
- Generated footage duration is decoupled from timeline edit duration: a model might generate a 5-second moving clip, but the `EditingDirector` trims it to a 2.8-second span (`sourceInMs`, `sourceOutMs`) aligned with the authoritative narration clock.
- The `EditDecisionList` (EDL) coordinates video cuts, audio bridges, transitions, and subtitle placements.

### 2.7 VieNeu Narration & FFmpeg Local Assembly
- VieNeu generates 48 kHz mono narration audio, aligned via WhisperX to provide the master audio clock.
- Desktop Electron main process renders the EDL using local FFmpeg, executing hard cuts and subtle scene transitions directly on moving video clips without synthetic zoompan.
- Post-render validation verifies audio-video synchronization and frame cadence (standard 720p / 24 FPS).
