# ADR-0030: Take / SelectedTake In/Out Trimming and Editing Decision List Pipeline

## Status

Accepted

## Context

In simple media pipelines, generated video clips are often placed directly onto a timeline at their full generated duration. This creates several major production issues:
1. Generative video models often produce artifacts at the very start (initial frame shock) or the very end (decay/jitter) of a clip.
2. The duration generated by a model (e.g., 5 seconds) rarely matches the precise rhythmic timing required by dialogue or narration (e.g., 2.7 seconds).
3. Directly feeding output generation into a timeline eliminates the possibility of multi-take comparison, selective re-generation, or quality gating.

## Decision

1. **Multi-Take Generation Architecture**:
   - A `Shot` does not hold raw video assets directly. Instead, every generation attempt produces an immutable `Take` entity.
   - Every `Take` undergoes automated `VideoQA` validation against an explicit 11-category failure taxonomy.
   - Retries are reason-aware, adjusting prompts, conditioning weights, or motion parameters based on the specific failure mode.

2. **SelectedTake Decoupling**:
   - The timeline consumes only `SelectedTake` value objects.
   - A `SelectedTake` binds a single approved `Take` to the parent `Shot` and specifies `sourceInMs` and `sourceOutMs`.
   - `timelineDurationMs = sourceOutMs - sourceInMs`.
   - The editor UI allows the user to switch takes and adjust trim handles without re-generating footage.

3. **EditingDirector & EditDecisionList (EDL)**:
   - An `EditingDirector` compiles all `SelectedTake` instances alongside the master narration clock (VieNeu) into an immutable `EditDecisionList`.
   - Local FFmpeg in the Electron desktop renderer executes video concatenation, audio muxing, subtitle burning, and color encoding based on the EDL.

## Consequences

- Completely eliminates "clip duration equals edit duration" assumptions.
- Allows trimming defective head/tail frames from generated AI footage.
- Supports both automatic best-take selection and manual user take selection.
